WHO WE ARE

SISIL Merchandise

SISIL Merchandise is the dedicated apparel sourcing division of SISIL Tejas India Private Limited, strategically based in South India’s textile hub – Tirupur, Tamil Nadu.

We connect global fashion brands with ethical, high-quality manufacturers across Tirupur, Coimbatore, Erode, Karur, and other key textile regions of Tamil Nadu.

With deep local roots and a global perspective, SISIL Merchandise is a trusted sourcing partner for international buyers seeking reliability, transparency, and innovation.

Our team brings decades of hands-on expertise across the entire supply chain—spanning manufacturing, sourcing, quality assurance, and logistics. Through long-standing partnerships with India’s leading garment manufacturers, we offer a comprehensive product range backed by consistent quality, compliance, and timely delivery. From concept to shipment, we accompany our clients through every stage of development—creating, implementing, and investing in solutions that make fashion more climate-conscious and commercially agile. Our Core Functions

01

Vendor Management

Identify and onboard manufacturers based on buyer needs. Negotiate pricing, timelines, and ensure compliance standards.

02

Product Development

Coordinate sampling, tech pack interpretation, and fabric sourcing. Suggest trims, washes, and finishes to optimize cost and quality.

03

Production & Quality Assurance

Conduct inline and final inspections. Ensure adherence to buyer specifications and global standards.

04

Logistics & Documentation

Manage shipping schedules, export documentation, and payments. Handle L/Cs, invoices, and customs coordination efficiently.

05

Client Communication

Act as a local representative for overseas brands. Provide regular updates, resolve issues, and ensure transparency.
